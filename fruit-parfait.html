Fruit Parfait

Layers of yogurt, fresh fruit, and crunchy granola for a light dessert or breakfast.

◷ 15 mins♜ Easy♙ 2 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Read the whole recipe before starting.
  • Prepare ingredients before cooking.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup strawberries
  • 1/2 cup blueberries
  • 1/2 cup granola
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Mint leaves (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Spoon a layer of yogurt into each glass.
  2. Add a layer of fruit and granola.
  3. Repeat the layers until the glass is filled.
  4. Drizzle a little honey over the top.
  5. Finish with mint and serve right away.
