Margherita Pizza

A classic pizza with tomato, mozzarella, basil, and a crisp homemade-style crust.

◷ 25 mins♜ Medium♙ 2–3 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Read the whole recipe before starting.
  • Prepare ingredients before cooking.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end.

Ingredients

  • 1 pizza base
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 cup fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 450°F (230°C).
  2. Spread tomato sauce evenly over the pizza base.
  3. Add mozzarella pieces across the top.
  4. Bake until the crust is crisp and the cheese is melted.
  5. Finish with basil and a drizzle of olive oil.
