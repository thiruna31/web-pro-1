About FlavorCanvas

A collection of simple, delicious and creative recipes for everyday cooking.

Our Story

FlavorCanvas started as a small idea: bring together simple, enjoyable recipes in one place. We wanted the site to feel useful first, with clear steps, familiar ingredients and a warm visual style.

The project is intentionally small so we can focus on clean HTML, strong CSS layouts, accessibility and responsive behavior.
