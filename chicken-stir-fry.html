Chicken Stir Fry

Quick chicken and colorful vegetables tossed in a savory stir-fry sauce.

◷ 20 mins♜ Easy♙ 2 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Read the whole recipe before starting.
  • Prepare ingredients before cooking.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end.

Ingredients

  • 250g chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper
  • 1 cup broccoli
  • 1 carrot
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat a pan or wok over medium-high heat.
  2. Cook the chicken until browned and cooked through.
  3. Add the vegetables and stir-fry until just tender.
  4. Add garlic, soy sauce, and sesame oil.
  5. Toss everything together and serve hot.
