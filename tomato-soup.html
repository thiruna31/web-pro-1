Tomato Soup

A smooth, comforting tomato soup with garlic, herbs, and a simple creamy finish.

◷ 25 mins♜ Easy♙ 4 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Read the whole recipe before starting.
  • Prepare ingredients before cooking.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end.

Ingredients

  • 4 large tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 2 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 cup cream
  • Salt, pepper, and basil

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat olive oil and soften the onion and garlic.
  2. Add tomatoes and cook until they begin to break down.
  3. Pour in the broth and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Blend until smooth, then stir in the cream.
  5. Season with salt, pepper, and basil before serving.
