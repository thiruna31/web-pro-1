Avocado Toast

Creamy avocado on crisp toast with lemon, herbs, and a simple seasoning mix.

◷ 30 mins♜ Easy♙ 2 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Read the whole recipe before starting.
  • Prepare ingredients before cooking.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning near the end.

Ingredients

  • 2 slices sourdough bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of salt
  • Black pepper
  • Chili flakes (optional)
  • Cherry tomatoes for topping

Preparation Steps

  1. Toast the bread until crisp and golden.
  2. Mash the avocado with lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  3. Spread the avocado mixture over the toast.
  4. Top with tomatoes and chili flakes if you like.
  5. Serve immediately while the toast is still crisp.
