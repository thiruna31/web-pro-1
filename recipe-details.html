Creamy Garlic Pasta

A rich and creamy pasta that's easy to make and perfect for any day. It pairs well with a fresh side salad.

◷ 30 mins♜ Easy♙ 2 servings

Cooking Tips

  • Use fresh garlic for better flavor.
  • Add spinach or mushrooms for extra vegetables.
  • Try a little chili flake if you like heat.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Preparation Steps

  1. Cook pasta according to package instructions.
  2. Heat olive oil in a pan and add garlic.
  3. Add cream and let it simmer for a few minutes.
  4. Mix in parmesan cheese and season.
  5. Combine with pasta and garnish with parsley.

Can I replace heavy cream?

Use half-and-half for a lighter sauce, but expect a thinner texture.
